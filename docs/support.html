Frequently Asked Questions

Is My Frontier free?
Yes — the core app is completely free. You can build a personalized ETF portfolio, view your Frontier Score, see growth projections, and chat with Alex the AI guide at no cost. My Frontier Premium ($4.99/month) unlocks unlimited portfolio saves, PDF export history, advanced stress-test scenarios, and removes all ads.
Is this real financial advice?
No. My Frontier is an educational tool that uses mathematical models to suggest ETF allocations based on your inputs. Nothing in the app constitutes personalized financial advice. All projections are estimates based on historical data and do not guarantee future results. We always recommend consulting a licensed financial advisor before making investment decisions.
How does My Frontier build my portfolio?
The app uses the Efficient Frontier — a Nobel Prize-winning mathematical model developed by Harry Markowitz in 1952. It downloads 10 years of real price data for ETFs in your selected categories, then runs mean-variance optimization to find the allocation with the best risk-adjusted return for your risk tolerance level. The same math institutional investors have used for decades, made accessible to everyone.
Why is my portfolio different each time I run it?
Your portfolio is calculated fresh each time using the latest available market data. As prices change daily, the optimal allocation shifts — so small differences between runs are expected and normal. Major differences would indicate a change in market conditions or a change in your selected categories or risk tolerance.
How do I cancel My Frontier Premium?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) — not by My Frontier directly. To cancel:

iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → My Frontier → Cancel
Android: Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → My Frontier → Cancel

You retain Premium access until the end of the current billing period.
I purchased Premium but the app still shows ads. What do I do?
Tap "Restore Purchases" on the Premium screen (accessible from the main menu). This re-validates your purchase receipt and should restore your Premium status immediately. If the problem persists, please email us with your Apple/Google order number.
