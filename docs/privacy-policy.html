My Frontier

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 6, 2026

My Frontier ("the app", "we", "our") is a free ETF portfolio optimizer for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We believe in plain English — no legalese.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We are intentionally minimal. My Frontier does not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your real investment portfolio, brokerage account, or financial data
  • Your social security number or any government ID
  • Precise GPS location
  • Any information from your contacts, camera, or microphone

No account is required to use My Frontier. You can build a portfolio, save it locally, and use the full app without ever providing personal information to us.

2. Information We Do Collect

The app collects the minimum needed to function:

  • Questionnaire inputs (stored on-device only): Your selected investment categories, risk tolerance (1–5), investment amount, and age — used solely to calculate your portfolio. This data lives in your device's local storage and is never sent to our servers or any third party.
  • App usage analytics (anonymous): Google AdMob and standard Expo telemetry may collect anonymized, aggregated data about how users interact with the app (e.g., which screens are visited, crash reports). This data cannot identify you personally.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

My Frontier uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID on iOS/Android) to serve personalized ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"

My Frontier Premium subscribers see no ads. Subscribing removes all AdMob inventory from the app.

4. AI Chat — Alex (Anthropic Claude)

The "Alex" AI assistant feature sends your conversation messages and your current portfolio allocation to Anthropic's Claude API in order to generate responses. This data is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Anthropic's data handling is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

We do not log, store, or retain your Alex conversation history on our servers beyond the duration of the active API request. Messages are not used to train AI models.

5. Market Data

Portfolio calculations use publicly available ETF price data fetched from Yahoo Finance. No personal data is transmitted in these requests — they are equivalent to looking up a stock price on a public website.

6. In-App Purchases — My Frontier Premium

Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not receive or store your payment card information. Purchase receipts are validated through RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing to delete on our end. To remove any locally stored portfolio data, simply uninstall the app. Your portfolio preferences are stored in your device's local storage and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

8. Children's Privacy

My Frontier is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at the address below and we will take steps to remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Not Financial Advice

My Frontier is an educational tool only. Nothing in the app or on this website constitutes financial advice. All portfolio suggestions are based on historical mathematical models and do not guarantee future returns. Always consult a licensed financial advisor before making investment decisions.
