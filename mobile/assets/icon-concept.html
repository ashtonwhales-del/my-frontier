My Frontier — App Icon Concept

SVG mountain icon · Export sizes: 1024 × 1024 px (App Store), 180 × 180 px (iPhone), 60 × 60 px (small)

1024 × 1024 — App Store
180 × 180 — iPhone home screen
60 × 60 — small / notification

Color Palette

  • Background (sky top): #0F1729 — navy black
  • Background (sky bottom / ground): #16213A — deep navy
  • Center mountain: #4361EE — brand blue
  • Left mountain: #312E81 — indigo dark
  • Right mountain: #3730A3 — indigo medium
  • Horizon line + peak diamond: #F59E0B — gold / amber

Export Instructions

  • Open this file in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
  • Right-click the 1024 × 1024 icon → "Save image as…" to get an SVG, or use a browser extension like "SVG Screenshot" to export as PNG.
  • Recommended: paste the SVG code into Figma or Sketch, export as PNG at 1× (1024 × 1024 px).
  • The App Store requires a 1024 × 1024 px PNG with no alpha channel (no transparency) and no rounded corners — Apple applies them automatically.
  • To convert SVG → PNG at exactly 1024 px, you can also use: npx sharp-cli --input icon.svg --output icon.png --width 1024 --height 1024

App Store Requirements Checklist

  • ✅ 1024 × 1024 px PNG
  • ✅ No alpha channel (no transparency)
  • ✅ No rounded corners (Apple clips them to superellipse)
  • ✅ No text or UI chrome (App Store guidelines)
  • ✅ Unique, recognizable at small sizes
  • ✅ High contrast — gold peak visible at 60 × 60 px

Design Notes

  • The three-mountain motif references the "frontier" metaphor directly — reaching a peak.
  • The gold horizon line ties to the Efficient Frontier curve visualization (the "efficient" boundary between risk and return).
  • The gold diamond at the peak is the "optimal point" on the Efficient Frontier — the portfolio with the best Sharpe ratio.
  • Deep navy background is consistent with the app's dark theme (#0F1729).
